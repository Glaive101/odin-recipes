Bobotie Recipe

Description

Bobotie is one the best, most nutritious, tasty meals to impress the family with a variety of ways to prepare to conform to your preferences.
Consisting out of substantial protein dense ingredients it serves as a great main meal.

Ingredients

  • 7x eggs
  • 500g minced meat
  • 1x onion
  • mushrooms
  • Ropertsons Spices
    • Mixed Herbs
    • Turmuric
  • double fat greek yogurt
  • mayonnaise
  • Mild Curry
  • Masala
  • Chutney
  • Lemon juice

Steps

  1. Add oil and one teespoon of butter, wait till melted.
  2. Cut and add the single onion.
  3. After 3 minutes cut and add mushrooms.
  4. When unions start showing a tint of brown add the minced meat.
  5. Add salt, mixed herbs and turmuric.
  6. Add mild curry and masala.
  7. Add Chutney and lemon juice.
  8. When minced meat does not have a shade of pink anymore, add yogurt and mayonnaise.
  9. Turn down to only maintaining a little heat for 10 minutes.
  10. Taste to see if you want more salt or sweet and adjust using salt and chutney.
  11. Turn of the heat and wait till cooled. Add 3 eggs to the mix and work it into the mix.
  12. Empty the mix into a cassarole, then add 4 eggs on top together with the leaves
  13. Cook in oven for 30 minutes at 180°.
